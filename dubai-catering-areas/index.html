Catering FAQ

Questions, answered

Which areas of Dubai does Shamiat cater?
All of Dubai, from Sheikh Zayed Road, DIFC, Business Bay and Downtown to Deira, Bur Dubai, Satwa, Al Wasl, Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, JLT, JVC, Dubai Hills, DAMAC Hills, DAMAC Hills 2, Meydan, Nad Al Sheba, Mirdif, Arabian Ranches and Jebel Ali. This page links to 58 community pages.
How many guests can you cater in Dubai?
From about 10 guests up to 1,000. Small gatherings get platters or a mezze table, and large weddings and corporate events get a full buffet with setup and service staff. Share your headcount and your area and we size the menu and quote.
Is Shamiat catering in Dubai halal?
Yes. Everything is halal and made fresh in our kitchen at Duja Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road. Every menu includes vegetarian mezze and mains.
