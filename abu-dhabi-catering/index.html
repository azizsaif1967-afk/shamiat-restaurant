Abu Dhabi catering FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you cater Syrian and Levantine food in Abu Dhabi?
Yes. Shamiat caters authentic halal Syrian and Levantine food for events across Abu Dhabi: office lunches, corporate events, weddings, birthdays, Ramadan iftar and suhoor, majlis gatherings and ADNEC exhibitions. We cook fresh in our Sheikh Zayed Road kitchen and drive the catering to your venue. WhatsApp +971 54 781 6266 for a quote.
Which areas of Abu Dhabi do you cater?
All of Abu Dhabi city and its suburbs, including Al Reem Island, Al Maryah Island and ADGM, Saadiyat, Yas Island, Al Raha, Khalifa City, Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Shakhbout City, Al Shamkha, Musaffah, the Corniche and ADNEC. For Al Ain or Al Dhafra, send your location and we will confirm.
How far ahead should I book catering in Abu Dhabi?
Book as early as you can. Abu Dhabi events are planned around the drive from our Dubai kitchen, so we confirm the lead time, delivery slot and any delivery charge together with your menu and quote.
What is the minimum order for Abu Dhabi catering?
Because every Abu Dhabi order is a dedicated drive, the minimum depends on your area and menu. Share your guest count and location and we will tell you straight away what works. Our spreads scale from small office lunches to banquets for hundreds of guests.
Do you cater office lunches and corporate events in Abu Dhabi?
Yes. We cater team lunches, board meetings, staff iftars, launches and conferences for offices in Al Maryah Island, ADGM, Al Reem, the Corniche, Khalifa City and Musaffah. Choose sharing platters, a hot buffet or a live charcoal grill station.
Can you cater exhibitions at ADNEC Abu Dhabi?
Yes. We cater exhibitor stands and teams at ADNEC during shows such as ADIPEC and IDEX: bulk mezze and grill platters, daily team lunches and post-show dinners. Send the show, hall, dates and headcount for a quote.
Can you cater weddings, parties and majlis gatherings in Abu Dhabi?
Yes. We cater weddings, engagements, birthdays, aqiqah and family majlis gatherings at villas, halls and venues across Abu Dhabi, with whole-lamb mansaf and oze, mezze tables, live grills and Shami sweets.
Is Shamiat catering halal, and do you have vegetarian options?
Yes. Everything is halal and made fresh. Every menu includes vegetarian mezze and mains, and we tailor the spread to dietary needs.
How do I get a catering quote for Abu Dhabi?
Fill the short form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send to +971 54 781 6266. You can also call that number or email shamiat.restaurant.ae@gmail.com. Include your date, guest count, area in Abu Dhabi and occasion.
