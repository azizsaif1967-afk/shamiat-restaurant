Hotel Guests & Concierges

Questions, answered

Do you deliver to hotels on Sheikh Zayed Road?
Yes — to every hotel on Sheikh Zayed Road and around the Trade Centre, including the Sheraton Grand, Fairmont, voco, Novotel WTC, Ibis WTC and One Central, Four Points SZR, Towers Rotana, Crowne Plaza SZR, Millennium Plaza, Rove Trade Centre, Dusit Thani, Shangri-La, Conrad, Jumeirah Emirates Towers, Sofitel Downtown, Holiday Inn DWTC and Grand Millennium. Shamiat is at Duja Tower, minutes from all of them.
How do I order food to my hotel room in Dubai?
The fastest way is to WhatsApp +971 54 781 6266 with your hotel name, room number and order — we confirm a delivery time directly. You can also call the same number, or order through Talabat, noon, Keeta, Smiles, Deliveroo or Careem. Ordering direct avoids the app fees.
How late can I order to my hotel?
Until 5:00 AM, every day. The kitchen serves the full menu right up to closing, so a late arrival at DXB or a late finish at the Trade Centre is well covered.
Is the food halal?
Yes. Shamiat is a fully halal Syrian and Levantine kitchen. Vegetarian options are available across the menu.
Can the concierge team add Shamiat to our recommended restaurants?
Yes, and we would welcome it. Call the restaurant on +971 54 781 6266 and we will send current menus, arrange priority handling for your guests, and set up a direct line for the concierge desk.
Can you deliver a group order to a suite or meeting room?
Yes. For groups, bulk mezze and charcoal-grill platters work much better than individual plates — they hold temperature, serve flexibly and cost less per head. Message us with the headcount and timing. Larger events are covered on the catering page.
How far is Shamiat from Dubai World Trade Centre?
About a two-minute walk. Shamiat is on the ground floor of Duja Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Trade Centre First — directly opposite the DWTC halls.

Staying nearby tonight?

Send us the hotel and room number on WhatsApp. Hot food, real Syrian kitchen, until 5 AM.
