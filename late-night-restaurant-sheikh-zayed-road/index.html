Questions, answered

What time does Shamiat close?
Shamiat is open every day from 12:00 PM until 5:00 AM. That is seven days a week, including weekends and public holidays. The kitchen serves the full menu right up to closing.
Is there a late-night restaurant open near Dubai World Trade Centre?
Yes — Shamiat, at Duja Tower on Sheikh Zayed Road, directly opposite the Dubai World Trade Centre. It is open until 5 AM daily, which makes it one of the few full-service options near DWTC after midnight.
Do you serve the full menu late at night?
Yes. There is no reduced late menu. The charcoal grill, the mezze section and the manakish oven all run until closing, so a 3 AM order is the same food as a 3 PM order.
Can you deliver to my hotel on Sheikh Zayed Road late at night?
Yes. We deliver to every Sheikh Zayed Road and Trade Centre hotel until closing — order direct on WhatsApp at +971 54 781 6266 with no extra app fees, or through Talabat, noon, Keeta, Smiles, Deliveroo or Careem. See the hotel delivery page for the full list.
Are you open for suhoor during Ramadan?
Yes. Because the kitchen already runs until 5 AM year-round, Shamiat serves suhoor without any change to normal hours. Call or WhatsApp ahead for larger groups, as Ramadan nights are busy.
Is the food halal?
Yes. Shamiat is a fully halal Syrian and Levantine kitchen, at every hour of service.
Do I need to book a table late at night?
For two to four people, usually not. For larger groups, and on show weeks at the Trade Centre, it is worth a quick WhatsApp ahead — the late hours are busier than people expect.

Hungry at an unreasonable hour?

The grill is on until 5 AM. Order direct on WhatsApp — no app, no extra fees — or just walk in.
