Catering FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you cater Syrian & Levantine food for events in Dubai?
Yes. Shamiat caters authentic Syrian and Levantine food anywhere in Dubai — weddings, corporate lunches, Ramadan iftar and suhoor, majlis and private gatherings, plus exhibition catering near Dubai World Trade Centre. All halal, made fresh, with custom menus and quotes.
What is the minimum number of guests?
Our Mezze Table starts from around 10 guests, and we scale up to large banquets and buffets for hundreds of guests. Share your headcount and we'll build a menu and quote to fit.
How do I book catering?
There's no online form — we plan every event personally. Send your date, guest count and occasion by WhatsApp on +971 54 781 6266, call the same number, or email shamiat.restaurant.ae@gmail.com, and we'll reply with a custom halal menu and quote.
Do you deliver across Dubai and set up on-site?
Yes. We deliver catering across Dubai and can provide live grill stations, chafing-dish or disposable service, and full buffet setup. We're at Duja Tower on Sheikh Zayed Road, opposite Dubai World Trade Centre, so exhibition and corporate teams nearby are especially quick to serve.
Can you cater exhibitions at Dubai World Trade Centre?
Yes. Shamiat is a short walk from DWTC and arranges group tables, bulk mezze and grill platters, and corporate lunch delivery for exhibition and conference teams during shows like GITEX, Gulfood and Arab Health. Share the show, dates and headcount for a custom quote.
Do you offer vegetarian and custom menus?
Yes. Every catering menu is built to your event — we include vegetarian mezze and mains, and tailor the spread for weddings, corporate, iftar or private dining. All dishes are halal.
