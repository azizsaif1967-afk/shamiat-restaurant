Catering FAQ

Questions, answered

What occasions does Shamiat cater?
Weddings and walima, nikah and katb al-kitab, engagements and mehndi nights, office parties, office farewells, canapés and small bites, birthday parties, corporate events and conferences, Ramadan iftar and suhoor, aqiqah, BBQ and live grill parties, and home parties, majlis and family functions.
Do you cater in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?
Yes. We cook in our kitchen at Duja Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai, and cater across Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and Ajman too.
How many guests can you cater in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?
From about 10 guests up to 1,000. Small gatherings get platters or a mezze table, and large weddings and corporate events get a full buffet with setup and service staff. Share your headcount and your location and we size the menu and quote.
Is Shamiat catering in Dubai and Abu Dhabi halal?
Yes. Everything is halal and made fresh in our kitchen at Duja Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road. Every menu includes vegetarian mezze and mains.
How do I book catering in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?
Fill the short form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send to +971 54 781 6266. You can also call that number or email shamiat.restaurant.ae@gmail.com with your date, guest count and location.
