- **Easy to order** — WhatsApp +971 54 781 6266, or 6 delivery apps to the room.

## Concierge offer (what to put on the table)
- **Concierge referral card** — a small card/QR for the desk: "Authentic Syrian,
  5 min away, open till 5 AM" → links to WhatsApp + menu PDF.
- **Guest perk** — a welcome item (e.g. complimentary Arabic sweet / mezze) on
  bookings that mention the hotel, or a small % off for referred guests.
- **Fast room delivery** — quote a realistic delivery window for their address.
- **Group / private dining** — a one-pager for corporate & event guests.
- Optional **referral commission** for confirmed group/catering bookings.
